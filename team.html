Clear data, so you can form your own convictions.

DisputeNews is a lightweight but data-intensive platform. It makes it quick and easy to reach advanced-level conclusions about the news without telling you which way to lean.

VA Founder

Vincent Julber Aguilera

Statistician · University of California, Riverside

Vincent began DisputeNews with a mission to give people clear data to build their own convictions from. As a statistician, he saw how much of the news is opinion layered on top of numbers, and set out to build a platform that puts the numbers first.

He leads the product end to end: how stories are grouped, how impact is measured, and how the platform stays lightweight while doing the heavy data work behind the scenes.

  • ProductWhat reaches the user, and how fast
