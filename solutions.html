Solutions

Clear data. Your conviction.

Our mission is to give people clear data to build their own convictions from. DisputeNews is a lightweight but data-intensive platform: we do the heavy lifting of collecting, grouping and measuring the news so you can quickly reach advanced-level conclusions without being told which way to lean.

Live Analytics

See what every story touches

Each story is mapped to the areas it affects, with the market signals moving around them.

  • Impacted areas. Companies, sectors, commodities and consumers, each marked as a direct, indirect or possible impact with a one-line reason.
  • Prediction market shifts. How the odds on related outcomes moved from before the story to now.
  • Options volume. Unusual jumps or drops in options activity compared with the normal daily average.
  • What to watch. Upcoming dates and decisions that could move the story next.
News Aggregate

Every side of the story

We group coverage from many outlets by topic, so you see the whole conversation instead of a single take.

  • Topic grouping. Related articles are clustered automatically into one story.
  • Side-by-side comparison. See how different outlets frame the same event.
  • Agreement and dispute. Spot where sources line up and where they disagree on the facts.
  • Transparent sources. Every story lists the outlets, filings and data it draws on.
Speed

From headline to impact in seconds

Our pipeline is optimized to get from a breaking article to useful data as fast as possible.

  • Fast intake. Articles are pulled in and grouped moments after they publish.
  • Instant mapping. Impacted areas and market signals are attached automatically.
  • Live updates. Results refresh as the story develops, so you’re never working from stale data.
  • Lightweight. Built to load quickly on your phone, with the heavy data work done behind the scenes.
Clear Presentation

Data first, no verdicts

We show you what changed and by how much, and leave the conclusion to you.

  • Direction at a glance. Green means a number went up and red means it went down, with stronger color for bigger moves.
  • Measured, not opinionated. No buy, sell or sentiment calls. Just the numbers and their context.
  • Built for conviction. Everything you need to form your own view, in one place.
