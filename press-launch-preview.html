DisputeNews Opens Launch Preview to Investors

Early investors can now try the lightweight, data-intensive news platform ahead of its public launch.

November 15, 2026

RIVERSIDE, Calif., November 15, 2026 — DisputeNews today announced that a launch preview of its software is now available to investors ahead of the platform’s public launch. The preview gives investors hands-on access to the core of DisputeNews: a lightweight but data-intensive platform that shows what each news story impacts, so readers can form their own convictions from clear data.

What’s in the launch preview

  • Live Analytics. Each story is mapped to the companies, sectors, commodities and consumers it affects, alongside the signals moving around them: shifts in prediction market odds, jumps in options volume and key dates to watch.
  • News Aggregate. Coverage from many outlets is grouped by topic, so readers can compare how sources frame the same event and see where they agree or dispute the facts.
  • Speed. The pipeline is built to go from a breaking headline to mapped impact in seconds, with results updating as a story develops.

“We built DisputeNews to give people clear data, not conclusions. The launch preview lets our investors see exactly how the platform turns breaking news into measurable impact, and how quickly it does it.”

— Vincent Julber Aguilera, Founder, DisputeNews

DisputeNews was founded by Vincent Julber Aguilera, a statistician at the University of California, Riverside. The project is advised by Dr. Aguilera, who holds a PhD in cellular and molecular biology and advises on research methods and data science.

Availability

Investors can request access to the launch preview through the DisputeNews Terminal or by emailing hello@disputenews.com. Client access will open at the official launch.

About DisputeNews

DisputeNews is a news platform built to give people clear data to form their own convictions. It shows which areas each story impacts and the market signals around them, without telling readers which way to lean.
