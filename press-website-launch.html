DisputeNews Launches Its Website, Opening Contact for Investors and Clients

The new site and domain give investors a direct way to get in touch and see platform previews, and give prospective clients a way to reach the team.

October 29, 2026

RIVERSIDE, Calif., October 29, 2026 — DisputeNews today launched its website and domain, giving investors and prospective clients a direct line to the company ahead of the platform’s public launch.

What the website offers

  • For investors. Investors can get in contact with the team, learn about the company and request previews of the DisputeNews platform.
  • For clients. Businesses and organizations interested in using DisputeNews can reach out to discuss their needs and how the platform could support them.
  • About the platform. The site introduces DisputeNews’ approach: a lightweight but data-intensive platform that shows which areas each news story impacts and the market signals around them, so people can form their own convictions from clear data.

DisputeNews was founded by Vincent Julber Aguilera, a statistician at the University of California, Riverside, and is advised by Dr. Aguilera, who holds a PhD in cellular and molecular biology and advises on research methods and data science.

How to get in touch

Investors and clients can contact DisputeNews at hello@disputenews.com. Media inquiries can be sent to press@disputenews.com.

About DisputeNews

DisputeNews is a news platform built to give people clear data to form their own convictions. It shows which areas each story impacts and the market signals around them, without telling readers which way to lean.
