Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small files stored on your device that help websites work and remember information about your visit.

2. Cookies we use

DisputeNews uses only strictly necessary cookies and similar storage. These are needed for the site to function — for example, remembering that you have seen our cookie notice.

We do not use advertising, tracking or analytics cookies.

3. Managing cookies

Because we only use necessary cookies, they cannot be switched off on the site. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, but parts of the site may not work as expected.

4. Changes to this policy

If we start using other types of cookies, we will update this policy. Choosing “Accept” on our cookie notice allows those optional cookies; choosing “Necessary only” limits us to strictly necessary ones.

5. Contact

Questions about cookies? Email hello@disputenews.com.
